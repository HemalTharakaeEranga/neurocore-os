# NeuroCore OS Trademark Policy

While the source code of NeuroCore OS is freely licensed under OSI-approved
open-source licenses and the visual artwork files are licensed under
CC-BY-SA-4.0, the names **"NeuroCore OS"**, **"NeuroCore"**, and the official
**penguin-and-gear emblem** (collectively, the "Marks") are trademarks of the
NeuroCore OS Project.

This policy protects users from confusing unofficial, modified, or non-free
derivatives with official NeuroCore OS releases while preserving full freedom
to fork, modify, and redistribute the software.

## Permitted Uses Without Prior Permission

1. **Unmodified Redistribution**: You may redistribute verbatim, unmodified
   official NeuroCore OS ISO, VDI, and QCOW2 images bearing the Marks.
2. **Nominative Fair Use**: You may truthfully refer to NeuroCore OS in
   documentation, benchmarks, reviews, compatibility statements ("Compatible with
   NeuroCore OS"), or educational materials.
3. **Community Advocacy**: Non-commercial community groups, tutorials, and
   events may display the Marks to promote official NeuroCore OS releases.

## Uses Requiring Rebranding (Modified Derivatives)

If you distribute a modified OS image, custom kernel build, or derivative that:

- bundles proprietary drivers, Modular MAX, NVIDIA CUDA, or third-party AI model
  weights by default; or
- alters core security, licensing, or build profiles beyond official upstream
  releases,

you **must** remove the NeuroCore OS Marks (including `/etc/os-release` `NAME`,
GRUB/Plymouth logos, and hicolor icons) and use a distinct product name. You may
factually state *"Based on NeuroCore OS"* in release notes.

## Prohibited Uses

- Implying official endorsement, certification, or sponsorship by the NeuroCore
  OS Project without written authorization.
- Registering domain names, social handles, or package namespaces intended to
  impersonate official NeuroCore OS channels.

For trademark permission inquiries, contact `trademarks@neurocore-os.org`.
